# How HimFit works

Plain-words notes on each piece, so you can explain (and change) any of it.

## 1. It's a PWA, not an app-store app
A **Progressive Web App** is a website that phones treat like an installed app. Two files make that happen,
both generated by `vite-plugin-pwa` from `vite.config.js`:
- **`manifest.webmanifest`** — name, icon, colours, "open full-screen". This is what makes Chrome offer *Install* and
  what iPhone uses for *Add to Home Screen*.
- **`sw.js` (service worker)** — a small script the phone keeps running in the background. It stores the app's files on
  the phone, so the app opens with no internet. With `registerType: 'autoUpdate'`, when a new version is deployed the
  phone downloads it in the background and uses it on the next open. That's how updates ship without reinstalling.

## 2. Offline-first data
Every tap saves to the phone first (`localStorage`) and marks that day as *dirty*. A sync step then pushes dirty days
to Supabase when there's a connection (`flushAll` in `src/app.js`), and pulls anything changed on the server since the
last pull (`pull`). If the same day was edited in two places, the newer edit wins (`updated_at`).
That's why logging never waits for the network.

## 3. Supabase: database + login
- **Auth:** email + password accounts (`signUp`, `signInWithPassword`, password reset).
- **Tables:** `days` (one row per user per day, the whole day as JSON) and `config` (targets, supplement changes,
  bloodwork). Schema: `supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql`.
- **Row Level Security (RLS):** rules inside the database that say "a user can only read or write rows where
  `user_id` is their own id". This is the real privacy guarantee — even if someone edited the app's code, the database
  would refuse to hand over anyone else's rows.
- **The publishable key** in `.env` is meant to be public (it ships inside every copy of the app).
  It only identifies the project; RLS decides what each signed-in person may touch.

## 4. Hosting on Vercel
Vercel watches the GitHub repo. Every push to `main` triggers `npm run build` and publishes `dist/` to the live URL
with HTTPS. Pull requests get their own preview URL. The app uses `#hash` addresses for its screens, so no
server routing config is needed.

## 5. How Claude stays connected
- **Code:** Claude pushes changes to this repo → Vercel redeploys → phones auto-update.
- **Data:** through the Supabase connector Claude can read your logs for analysis and update the `config` row
  (targets, supplement schedule, bloodwork). The app pulls those changes the next time it opens.

## 6. The nutrition engine
`src/data.js` holds ingredients per 100 g (macros from pack labels; micronutrients estimated from IFCT 2017 / USDA).
Each food is a recipe of ingredient grams; a day's total is the sum of its items, drinks and **ticked** supplements.
Processed-food share = calories from NOVA-4 (ultra-processed) ingredients ÷ total calories.

## 7. Insights are fixed statistics
For each pair (e.g. bong hits → next night's sleep) the app takes every day where both are logged.
With 14+ paired days it computes Pearson's *r*; if |r| ≥ 0.4 it compares days above vs at-or-below the median
and writes one fixed sentence. Same data → same result, every time.

## 8. Secrets rule
`.env` is committed because both values are public. Anything secret (a service-role key, an API key with write
powers) must **never** go in this repo or in the app. It would belong in Vercel's environment variables or a
Supabase Edge Function secret.

## Profile and targets (v1.3)
All maths lives in `src/profile.js` as plain functions, separate from the screens.
- **BMR:** Mifflin–St Jeor: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5 (men) / −161 (women).
- **Maintenance (formula):** BMR × daily-movement factor (1.25 sitting, 1.4 some, 1.55 a lot) + training (3.8 kcal × kg × hours per session × sessions ÷ 7).
- **Maintenance (measured):** average logged intake over the last 28 days − weight-trend slope × 7,700 kcal/kg. Needs 14 complete days and 8 weigh-ins over 10+ days. Blended in from 50% (14 days) to 100% (28 days). Ignored if it is more than 25% away from the formula (usually a logging gap).
- **Target:** maintenance + pace × 7,700 ÷ 7 (deficit capped at 25%). Protein g/kg by goal, fat 0.8 g/kg minimum and 30% maximum, carbs are the rest, fibre 14 g per 1,000 kcal, water 35 ml/kg + training.
- **Frozen weekly:** targets for a Monday–Sunday week use only data from before that Monday.
- **Body fat:** US Navy tape method (waist + neck, plus hips for women). Shown only if measured in the last 60 days. FFMI = lean kg ÷ m², adjusted to 1.8 m.

## Energy score (v1.4)
`src/energy.js`: plain functions, fixed rules, no AI. Base 75 plus eight layers, clamped 5–98, computed every 10 minutes from wake to bedtime. Green 70+, yellow 45–69, red under 45.
Inputs: morning check-in (bed/wake times, sleep quality, mood, body feel), food with times, drinks with times (caffeine model: absorbs in ~45–60 min, half-life 5 h), water taps with times, bong-hit taps with times (tolerance from the 7-day average), last night's drinks and late hits.
Each morning you rate yesterday (1–5). The app stores its own prediction for yesterday next to it (`ePred` vs `eRep`), and Trends shows how often they match. The numbers get tuned monthly from that comparison.

## v1.5 additions
- **Weight at any time of day** (`src/profile.js`): corrected to a morning-equivalent by hours since waking: 0–2 h none, 2–5 h −0.4%, 5–9 h −0.8%, 9–13 h −1.2%, 13 h+ −1.4%. Raw and corrected both stored; trends, maintenance and targets use the corrected one.
- **Bong-hit timing** (`src/hits.js`): taps less than 3 minutes apart form a chain. 1–4 = one session at that moment. 5+ = catch-up: spread back from now at 6 an hour, never before your last logged hit or wake time, never denser than 10 an hour. Override: last hour / last 2–3 h / since I woke.
- **Sleep** comes only from bed and wake times (morning card or Check-in). Hours are never typed.
- **Day tags** (exam, sick, party, travel, stressed) feed the energy score; sick and travel days are left out of maintenance and insights.
- **Energy model v1 is frozen until 16 Dec 2026**; every stored prediction carries its version (`eVer`).
- **Target change log**: when the weekly target moves, Me shows one line with the reason and it is saved to the change history.
- **Weekly review** (Today on Mondays, Trends always): averages, weight change, energy, sleep, substances, low-all-week nutrients, and one rule-based change for the week.
- **Export** (tap the sync status): days, food and drinks as CSV, plus a full JSON backup.

## Tests
`npm test` runs `tests/unit.test.mjs` (maths) and `tests/app.test.cjs` (the whole app in a headless browser with a fake Supabase).

## Naps (v1.5.1)
Check-in → "Naps & daytime sleep": add start and end times (any number per day). They add to the day's sleep total (Check-in, Today, weekly review, export `nap_h`) and count 70% toward sleep debt. In the energy score: no score while asleep (the curve breaks); grogginess after waking (6–15 points for 10–40 min, by nap length); a lift up to +10 that fades over ~5 h; part of the 'hours awake' clock resets (a 2 h nap resets 80%); time asleep is left out of 'food or water expected by now', and fasting time counts 30% while asleep.

## Tape measurements (v1.5.3)
Six spots, whenever you like (Me tab → Measurements → Update measurements, with a date; not in Check-in or the profile form): waist (navel), neck, chest (nipple level), upper arm (right, flexed), mid-thigh (right), calf (right). Hips too for women.
- Body fat = average of the US Navy method (waist + neck) and Relative Fat Mass (RFM = 64 − 20 × height ÷ waist for men, 76 − … for women). Both are shown. Lean mass and FFMI use the average.
- Measurements card on Me: each spot's change since the first measurement, plus waist-per-kg: cm of waist gained per kg gained. ≤0.7 mostly lean, 0.7–1.0 watch, >1.0 the surplus is too big.
- No reminder: measure whenever you choose.

## v1.7.0 — Energy ratings, day thirds, moments, real bedtime
- **Real bedtime:** once the next morning's check-in gives your bedtime, that day's curve ends there, and the stored prediction (ePred) averages only the hours you were awake. Before, it ran to your usual profile bedtime.
- **Energy ratings:** a card on Today asks "How's your energy right now?" (Awful–Great = 1–5) 3, 7 and 11 h after your wake-up. Skippable; each prompt stays until the next one is due (or 16 h awake). Saved as ci.er [{s: slot, v: 1–5 or null if skipped, t}]. Shown as ◆ on the energy chart.
- **Day thirds:** the three tiles are Early day / Midday / Late day — your waking day (wake to bedtime) cut into equal thirds, with the time range under each. The part you're in shows "so far" (only hours already lived); later parts show "forecast".
- **Moments (Check-in):** 🚿 Hot shower, 🚿 Cool shower, 🌶️ Spicy time — tap when it happens, time editable, removable. Saved as ci.ev [{id, k, t}]. Shown as markers on the chart. Not in the score: tested against ratings on 16 Dec, together with heat (from historical Ahmedabad weather, nothing recorded daily).
- The score's maths (model v1) is unchanged.

## v1.8.0 — Energy model v2 (19 Nov 2026, frozen until 16 Dec)
New layers on top of v1 (v1 layers unchanged):
- **Moments:** cool shower +7 (peak 5 min, gone 75 min); hot shower +3 for 20 min then −4 (peak 50 min, gone 110 min); 🌶️ +3 for 20 min then −7 (peak 45 min, gone 2 h).
- **Patterns:** last meal (≥100 kcal) <2 h before bed −3 next morning (<1 h −5); caffeine level at bedtime >40 mg → −level/20 (max −6); both ×0.5 when the morning report is answered, fading by 6 h / 8 h awake. Nights 4–7 back: mean shortfall under 7.5 h × 1.5 (max −5) all day. Low mood (Low/Irritable) or bad sleep (Terrible/Poor) 3+ mornings running: −3 each.
- **Heat:** Ahmedabad monthly average high (Jan–Dec 28,31,36,40,42,38,33,32,33,35,32,29 °C); above 34 °C: −0.6 per °C (max −5) on an 11 AM–8 PM hump peaking 3 PM. Fan and cooler assumed. Zero Oct–Feb.
- **Your energy checks:** the latest rating in the last 4 h pulls the score toward the rating's band middle (Awful 20, Low 42, Okay 57, Good 72, Great 88): 70% at the rating time, linearly to 0 at 4 h. The stored daily prediction (ePred) is computed without ratings, so it stays a fair test.
All raw logs are kept, so v1 and v2 can both be re-run over the same days on 16 Dec and scored against the ratings.

## v1.8.1 — Heat removed, gentler energy checks (still model v2, frozen until 16 Dec)
- Heat layer removed (user's call).
- Energy checks are a nudge, not an override: target = your rating's band middle minus your personal bias (mean of rating score − model score over your last 30 checks, once there are 10+; each check now stores the model's score at that moment as `m`). Within ±8 of the model: no change. Beyond: 30% of the gap past 8, halved if the model is ≤40 and you rated higher or ≥80 and you rated lower, capped ±10, fading linearly to 0 over 3 h. Stored predictions still exclude it.

## v1.14.0 — Training (third pillar)
